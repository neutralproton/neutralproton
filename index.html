inexio

Passive stabilization of respiratory rhythm using distributed tactile phase fields.

inexio is a closed-loop system that estimates respiratory rhythm stability using motion-derived proxies and introduces a non-instructional, phase-biased tactile signal to reduce rhythm volatility over time.

Explore in depth

The system avoids explicit breathing instructions, visual pacing, or auditory cues. Instead, it computes a Respiratory Rhythm Volatility Index (RRVI) derived from inertial motion features such as cycle variance, phase jitter, and asymmetry.

When sustained instability is detected, a distributed haptic field is generated across multiple actuators embedded in a necklace form factor. The tactile signal exhibits lower volatility than the detected rhythm and propagates spatially in a phase-biased manner aligned with inhale, hold, and exhale phases.

As rhythm stability improves, intervention magnitude is reduced. The system is not diagnostic, not instructional, and not a medical treatment device.

CryoFlux

Modular thermal buffering using inert working gas circulation.

(CryoFlux flow animation placeholder)

CryoFlux is a conceptual thermal management system that uses inert working gas circulation within a sealed modular block to absorb, redistribute, and reject heat without electricity, liquid coolant, or ice generation.

Explore in depth

Heat input enters the system via conduction into a structured internal lattice. An inert working gas undergoes controlled pressure and density changes as it traverses the module, transporting thermal energy toward a passive rejection surface.

The architecture is explicitly non-cryogenic and avoids phase-change materials, pumps, compressors, or active control systems. All operation is driven by thermal gradients and internal geometry.

CryoFlux exists as a venture-scale exploration of whether modular, gas-mediated thermal buffering can reduce reliance on electrically powered cooling in constrained environments.

Ultrasonic Solar

Photovoltaic augmentation via mechanically coupled ultrasonic excitation.

(Ultrasonic Solar excitation animation placeholder)

Ultrasonic Solar is a photovoltaic augmentation concept that applies low-amplitude, high-frequency mechanical excitation to the underside of conventional solar panels. The system is designed as a retrofit layer, not a replacement architecture.

Explore in depth

One or more ultrasonic transducers are mechanically coupled to the panel undercarriage, with diffusion rods assisting in even propagation of excitation across the substrate.

The excitation is intended to explore two effects: qualitative interaction with charge transport pathways and continuous surface agitation that discourages particulate adhesion.

The project explicitly avoids claims of guaranteed efficiency gain. Evaluation is framed as a qualitative balance between energy input and recovered output, pending technical validation.

muscleOS

Set-by-set adaptive training intelligence from subjective effort signals.

muscleOS is a fitness analytics concept that learns from self-reported effort, repetition count, and time to model individualized adaptation patterns over repeated training sessions.

Explore in depth

Rather than prescribing programs, muscleOS observes how users experience training. Over time, it identifies patterns between perceived effort, performance decay, and recovery behavior.

These learned patterns can be integrated into future training suggestions, load adjustments, or recovery pacing, enabling workouts that improve without rigid programming or external coaching dependency.
