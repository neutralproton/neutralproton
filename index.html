inexio

Passive Respiratory Rhythm Stabilization

Systems and Methods for Passive Stabilization of Respiratory Rhythm Using Distributed Tactile Phase Fields

inexio is a closed-loop human–machine interaction system that stabilizes respiratory rhythm volatility through non-instructional, spatially distributed tactile entrainment.

The system does not guide breathing.
It does not issue commands.
It establishes a tactile phase field that the body subconsciously entrains to.

Respiratory Rhythm Volatility Index (RRVI)

Respiratory stability is evaluated using a motion-derived volatility metric computed over rolling windows.

RRVI reflects rhythm stability, not rate, correctness, or optimality.

When sustained volatility exceeds adaptive thresholds, tactile intervention is introduced. As RRVI decreases, intervention magnitude is reduced. When stability is restored, intervention may cease entirely.
